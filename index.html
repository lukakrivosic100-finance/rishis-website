A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

INDIA'S CRICKET STORY

From the first steps in international cricket to becoming a force across every format, India's story is full of determination and iconic performances.

1932

The first Test

India played its first Test match at Lord's against England, beginning its journey in international cricket.

1983

A historic triumph

Kapil Dev led India to its first Cricket World Cup title, defeating the West Indies at Lord's.

2007

World T20 champions

India won the inaugural ICC Men's T20 World Cup in South Africa under MS Dhoni.

2011

World Cup at home

India lifted the ODI World Cup in Mumbai, becoming champions on home soil.

THE NAMES THAT INSPIRED A NATION

INDIAN CRICKET LEGENDS

Batters, bowlers, captains and all-rounders whose talent and achievements helped shape the game.

ST

Sachin Tendulkar

Master Blaster · Batter

One of cricket's greatest batters, celebrated for his extraordinary consistency and record-breaking career.

KD

Kapil Dev

World Cup captain · All-rounder

Led India to its landmark 1983 World Cup victory and was one of the game's finest all-rounders.

MS

MS Dhoni

Captain · Wicketkeeper

Calm under pressure, he captained India to the 2007 T20 and 2011 ODI World Cup titles.

VK

Virat Kohli

Modern great · Batter

Known for remarkable run-scoring, intensity and match-winning performances across formats.

RD

Rahul Dravid

The Wall · Batter

Admired for technique, patience and a distinguished international career.

SC

Sunil Gavaskar

Opening batter

A trailblazing opener who set new standards for batting against fast bowling.

YS

Yuvraj Singh

All-rounder

A key figure in India's 2007 and 2011 World Cup triumphs, famous for explosive batting.

JB

Jasprit Bumrah

Fast bowler

A world-class fast bowler known for accuracy, unusual action and control at the death.

THE FORMATS OF CRICKET

Different challenges, different strategies — one game loved around the world.

THE CLASSIC

Test Cricket

Played over up to five days, Test cricket rewards patience, skill, stamina and strategy.

THE ONE-DAY GAME

One Day Internationals

Each side faces up to 50 overs, balancing careful batting with attacking cricket.
